01 Process
01

Read the teams

Every row of the CSV is validated as it is read. A blank name or a missing column stops the run and reports the exact row.

02

Solve the slots

Each run becomes a decision variable bounded by the number of slots. The solver searches until every constraint holds.

03

Lay out the day

Slots are converted to clock times from your start, then written into a time-by-table grid.

02 Model

Three hard constraints

The schedule is not assembled row by row. It is described as a set of rules the solver is not allowed to break, and any timetable it returns satisfies all of them at once.

  1. Three runs per team Every team receives exactly three runs across the day.
  2. All runs in different slots A team is never scheduled on two tables at the same time.
  3. Slot capacity is the table count A slot never holds more matches than you have tables.
